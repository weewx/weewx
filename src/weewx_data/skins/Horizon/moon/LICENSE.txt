Moon Phase and Libration, 2026 - https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5587/

Works of the US federal government are in the public domain (17 U.S.C. Sec. 105).
Rendered from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter elevation data.

32 frames, one for each 1/32 of the synodic month (29.530588 days). The phase is
right in any year. The libration, the slight rocking that shows a little around the
limb, was computed for 2026 and drifts thereafter by an amount nobody will see.

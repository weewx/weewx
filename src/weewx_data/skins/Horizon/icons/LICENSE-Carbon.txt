The SVG files in this directory are from the IBM Carbon Design System.

    https://github.com/carbon-design-system/carbon

They are distributed under the Apache License, Version 2.0:

    http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

Each file has had its <title> and its transparent backing rectangle removed, and
its whitespace collapsed. The paths themselves are unchanged.

They came here by way of weewx-wdc by David Baetge, which uses the same set:

    https://github.com/Daveiano/weewx-wdc

The weather symbols in icons/forecast/ come from the same set. They are named
here for what they show rather than for the code they came from: clear.svg,
partly-cloudy-night.svg, thunderstorm.svg, and so on, which is the vocabulary
WMO 4677 uses once it has been reduced to pictures.
